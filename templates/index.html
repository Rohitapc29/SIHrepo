How it works

Data preprocessing

We clean and normalize skills, roles and company info from internship listings so that synonyms like "AI" and "Artificial Intelligence" are treated the same.

Profile matching

Your education, skills, interests, and location preferences are converted into a weighted text profile using TF-IDF vectors.

Recommendations

We compute similarity between your profile and internship postings, ranking the best matches and displaying them as simple, neat cards.
